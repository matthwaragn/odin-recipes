Pizookie

Description

A pizookie (pizza‑cookie) is a warm, gooey, oversized chocolate‑chip cookie baked in a skillet so the edges turn golden while the center stays soft and molten. It’s usually served straight from the pan with scoops of ice cream melting over the top.

Cream butter and sugars, then mix in an egg and vanilla. Stir in flour, baking soda, salt, and chocolate chips. Press the dough into a small oven‑safe skillet and bake until the edges are set but the center is still soft. Serve warm with ice cream.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup chocolate chips
  • Vanilla ice cream for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a small cast‑iron skillet (6–8 inches).
  2. Whisk melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth. Add egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips.
  4. Spread dough into the skillet and bake 18–20 minutes until golden at the edges and soft in the center.
  5. Top with vanilla ice cream and enjoy straight from the skillet.
